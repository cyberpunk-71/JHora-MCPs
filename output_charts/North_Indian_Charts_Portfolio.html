3. Pt. P.V.R. Narasimha Rao Video & JHora Method Explanation

In his definitive video lecture on Divisional Charts (Part 6) and research papers on Two Novel Transit Principles, Pt. P.V.R. Narasimha Rao explicitly clarifies:

"Similarly for Dashamsa, I use this variation, the third one in Jagannatha Hora which is Parasara Dashamsa with even sign reversal, go reverse and end in nine... But for Taurus, Parasara taught that you count from the ninth house. So what people normally do is they start from Capricorn and go forward. But what I found is you have to go in reverse... So the first 1/10 of Taurus is mapped to Libra, and the last part is mapped to Cancer. This is actually reversed."

Under this exact formulation in Jagannatha Hora:
• D-10 Lagna falls in Virgo (8th division of Aquarius counted from itself).
• Saturn in Taurus (even sign, 8th division) is reckoned in reverse and lands in Aquarius (6th House).
• Ketu in Sagittarius (odd sign, 3rd division) lands in Aquarius (6th House) with Saturn.
• D-24 Lagna falls in Aquarius (19th division of Aquarius counted from Leo).
